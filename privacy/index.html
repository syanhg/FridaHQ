Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026

MonyCompany Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates Frida, a personal agent for iPhone. Frida keeps your conversation, and what it knows about you, on your phone. This policy explains the little that reaches us, what leaves your phone and why, and what you can ask us to do about any of it.

1. Information we collect

  • Sign in with Apple. Frida offers no other way in. We request your name only, not your email address. Apple also returns a stable identifier for your account. Both are held in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Purchases. If you buy something through Frida, our server keeps what is needed to place and track the order: what you bought, its price, and its status. Your name, email, phone number, and delivery address are kept only until the order has been placed or has failed. If you connect a payment wallet, our server holds the connection so it can request payment when you approve a purchase. Card numbers are never stored by us and never reach your phone.
  • Support and access requests. What you write to us, and the address you write from.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and performance reports, if you have allowed your device to share them. These reach us through Apple, aggregated, and do not contain your conversation.

Frida has no cookies, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs.

2. What stays on your device

Your conversation, the memories Frida forms about you, the habits it observes, the cards it draws, the work it has scheduled, your reservations, and its cache of photographs are written to the app’s private storage on your phone. This includes anything Frida reads for you from an account you connect, which becomes part of the conversation. Your Apple account identifier, your name, the contact and delivery details you save, and the tokens for the accounts you connect are held in the iOS Keychain on your phone. None of it is uploaded to us, and we cannot recover it for you.

3. What leaves your device, and why

Frida needs outside services to answer you. Each receives only what its part of the job requires:

  • AI model providers receive your message, the conversation so far, the standing memories Frida holds about you, and the results of anything it looked up or read for you, including from accounts you connect. During a voice call, they also receive your voice and the text of Frida’s answers so they can be spoken. A photo is sent only if you attach it.
  • Search, map, and weather services receive a search query, the address of a page, or the name of a place. If you allow location, they may receive your location so “nearby” means nearby and a forecast is for where you are.
  • Accounts you connect receive the requests Frida makes on your behalf, with the permission you granted.
  • Shopping and payment partners receive what you are buying, your name, email, and delivery address, and the payment you approved, so the store can fulfil the order.
  • Restaurant booking partners receive what you are looking for and, when you book, your name, phone number, email, and a card if the restaurant requires one. These go to the restaurant’s booking system.

We use AI model providers under terms that do not allow them to train their models on what we send, and we switch off storage of requests wherever a provider allows it. Your precise location is never sent to the model. It is told the name of your neighbourhood, and nothing finer. What these providers do with what they receive is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Gmail and Google Calendar

If you connect Gmail or Google Calendar, Frida asks Google for read-only access and nothing wider. It cannot send, delete, label, or change email, and it cannot create, change, or delete calendar events.

  • What Frida accesses. Gmail: the messages and conversations it searches or opens to answer you. Google Calendar: your list of calendars and the events on them.
  • How it is used. Only to provide the features you ask for: answering questions about your email and your schedule, showing messages and events in the conversation, drafting a reply you send yourself, and showing your Google events alongside the calendar on your phone.
  • Where it goes. Frida reads your Google data from your phone, directly from Google. What it reads is sent to our AI model providers to produce your answer, and during a voice call the answer is sent to be spoken. It is never stored on our servers.
  • What we never do. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use it for advertising or to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. Nobody at MonyCompany reads it, unless you ask us to for a specific message, it is needed to investigate abuse or keep Frida secure, or the law requires it.
  • How to revoke access. Tap Disconnect beside Gmail or Calendar in the app, or remove Frida at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Delete Account in the app revokes both.

Frida’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Permissions the app asks for

Each is asked for when it is first needed, never at launch, and Frida works without any of them. Once iOS has an answer on file, only Settings can change it.

  • Calendar and Reminders, to answer questions about your week, put holds on your calendar, and keep your lists in sync.
  • Location, as described above. Optionally always, so an errand tied to arriving somewhere works with the app closed.
  • Microphone, only while you are on a voice call with Frida.
  • Photos, to show your latest photos above the keyboard. A photo leaves your phone only if you attach it.
  • Motion, to count steps while you track a walk or a run. It stays on your phone.
  • Face ID, to confirm it is you before a payment or a booking. iOS handles this; Frida never sees your face data.
  • Apple Music, to play what you ask for.
  • Notifications, for timers, moments you asked about, and work you scheduled.

6. How we use it

To let you sign in and keep you signed in; to run what you ask Frida to run, including placing orders and bookings you approve; to answer you when you write to us; to fix crashes and improve the app; to investigate reports of abuse; and to meet legal obligations.

7. How we share it

  • Service providers described in section 3, and the company that hosts our server, only for the purposes described there.
  • Apple, which handles sign-in, app distribution, and any diagnostics you have chosen to share.
  • Legal authorities, where the law requires it, and an acquiring party in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

8. Connected accounts

You choose which accounts to connect. Each is connected through the provider’s own sign-in, so you authorize with that provider and grant only the permissions you are shown. The tokens are stored in your device’s Keychain and used from your device. You can disconnect an account at any time in the app, or revoke it in that provider’s own settings.

9. Retention and deletion

Delete Account in the app (the + button, then the row with your name on it) erases your conversation and every thread, the memories and habits Frida formed, work it had scheduled, your saved details, the grants for every connected account, anything it put on your Lock Screen, its notifications, and its cache of photographs. Deleting the app removes the same material from your phone.

Records of purchases, and a payment wallet connection if you made one, are kept on our server until you ask us to delete them. Write to us and we will. We keep support correspondence until we have finished acting on it.

Calendar events and reminders Frida wrote for you stay in your own apps. Deleting an account is not licence to reach into them.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit, keep credentials and tokens in the iOS Keychain, and keep your content on your device rather than on a server of ours. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because your data lives on your device, the passcode, Face ID, and backup settings on that device are part of protecting it.

11. Your rights

You can delete what is on your phone yourself, as described in section 9. You can also ask us to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold. Depending on where you live you may have further rights over how your data is used. Write to seungyong@usemony.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

12. Children’s privacy

Frida is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will remove it.

13. International transfers

We operate from the United States. Information that reaches us, and requests your device makes to the providers in section 3, may be processed in the United States or anywhere those providers operate. Where the law requires it, we apply appropriate safeguards to those transfers.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and change the date above, and we will tell you directly if the change is a material one.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy: seungyong@usemony.com.
