Frida is a personal agent that lives somewhere between thought and motion. Its interface flows like water, taking shape around your intent rather than asking you to conform to its own.

Fluid by design.The interface bends to you.

Panels, prompts and controls assemble around the task in front of you, then dissolve when it's done. Nothing to arrange, nothing to put away.

A step ahead.It reads intent, not commands.

Frida picks up on where a piece of work is heading and prepares the next move, so the thing you were about to ask for is already waiting.

Always a keystroke away.Summon it from anywhere.

One shortcut brings Frida into whatever you're already in — a document, a browser tab, a terminal. It arrives with the context intact.

Private by default.Your context stays yours.

What Frida learns about your work belongs to you. Nothing is sold, nothing is traded, and you can clear its memory whenever you like.

Finishes the job.It acts, it doesn't just answer.

Give Frida a goal and it carries the work through — the calls, the edits, the follow-ups — checking back with you only where a decision is genuinely yours.

One continuous thought.Every device, same thread.

Start something on your phone and pick it up on your desk mid-sentence. Frida keeps one thread of work rather than a copy on each screen.
